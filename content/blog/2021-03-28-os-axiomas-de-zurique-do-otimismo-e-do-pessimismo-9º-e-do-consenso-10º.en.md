#### THE 9th MAJOR AXIOM: ON OPTIMISM AND PESSIMISM

"Optimism means expecting the best, but confidence means knowing how you will handle the worst. Never make a move if you are merely optimistic."

You need to be very careful with optimism, because it can be dangerous. When money is involved, what you need is confidence, which comes from the constructive use of pessimism. Never make a move based on optimism alone; instead, look for confidence - confidence that doesn't come from expecting the best, but from knowing how to handle the worst. Before getting into a deal, think about how you'll get out of it if it goes wrong.

----

#### THE 10th MAJOR AXIOM: ON CONSENSUS

"Disregard the majority opinion. It is probably wrong."

We need to be aware that the majority isn't always right, and that it's more likely for a few to be right than many. Don't let yourself be swayed by the herd: think for yourself, question, study, examine and challenge old concepts.

##### THE 14th MINOR AXIOM

Never follow speculative fads. Often, the best time to buy something is when nobody else wants it.

When lots of people want to buy something, the price tends to go up because of high demand. When nobody wants it, the price usually drops so that the sale can happen.
