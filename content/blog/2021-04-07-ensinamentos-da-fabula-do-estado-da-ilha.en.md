The island state fable is a video series (in Portuguese) that explains in a simple way how the economy works. As an analogy to understand economic concepts, it uses an island as the example.

- - -

### 1. Basic concepts

We'll call it the **Island State**. It has 100 inhabitants. Some are fishermen, some harvest bananas, others build canoes, etc. Right now it has no currency, so purchases are made by barter: bananas for fish, and so on.

Seeing the need for a state to exist - for example, to guarantee private property and provide security and health services - the state is officially created. A president (Executive), a judge (Judiciary) and 3 people to make the rules (Legislative) are elected. On top of that, a currency is created for the state, which we'll call the stone unit.

Coins are then issued in the same proportion as the island's domestic production (GDP). If the island produced 100 bananas and 50 fish, for example, 150 stones were issued.

> **Concept: GDP**
>
> According to IBGE (Brazil's statistics bureau): gross domestic product is the sum of all final goods and services produced. It measures only final goods and services to avoid double counting. If a country produces 100 reais of wheat, 200 reais of wheat flour and 300 reais of bread, for example, its GDP will be 300 reais, because the values of the flour and wheat are already embedded in the value of the bread.
>
> Practical examples of Brazil's GDP:
>
> 2019: 7.257 trillion reais
> 2020: 7.4 trillion reais

Because of the new positions created (president, judge, police, etc.), a tax is created to support the state: a 20% tax on production.

> **Concept: Tax**
>
> A tax is a mandatory levy charged by the government. It's an amount you pay that helps cover the State's administrative expenses.

Besides creating taxes, the state has another option to meet its needs: it can issue debt securities. Basically, savers lend money to the state, and that's how public debt is created.

> **Concept: Public debt**
>
> It's the debt the government takes on to finance expenses it can't pay with tax revenue or other sources of income.

With the creation of currency, it's now possible to save and lend your wealth. Savers want their wealth to grow with interest, and people who need money - to expand their businesses, for example - borrow it and pay interest. In other words, the creation of currency makes trade easier, enables expansion and opportunities, and encourages saving.

That's how the **bank** (an intermediary) was created, allowing idle resources to become productive.

- - -

### 2. What causes inflation and why do interest rates go up?

As the island evolves, new professions appear. For example, consumers used to have to buy directly from producers, until an intermediary between them was created: the supermarket.

That's how transport services appeared to bring products to the market (suppliers), among other services. These new businesses increased the island's production.

The government then sees an opportunity to create a public service to protect the island's environment. It creates positions like environmental police and adds more judges to help enforce the laws. With that, the government increases its expenses, but not its revenue.

**Concept:** Public deficit is the name given to the situation where total public expenses are greater than total public revenue.

**Concept:** Primary deficit - when interest expenses and monetary adjustment are excluded from the deficit calculation.

The government is running a deficit and decides to raise taxes even further: the market, which used to make a profit, is now paying a lot of taxes and not profiting. So it decides to stop buying from the supplier and buy directly from the producer. The supplier company has to shut down. The market lays off some employees. A crisis begins.

> **Concept: Economic recession**
>
> An economic recession happens when a country's economy contracts. More precisely, when gross domestic product (GDP) stops growing and starts to fall.

**IMPORTANT!** Did you notice that at no point did the state shrink? At no point during the crisis did the state "suffer" from the crisis's problems.

- - -

### 3. Exchange rate

The island was suffering from the crisis... high interest rates and little purchasing power. Until a boat with people from another island (the Northern State) showed up.

Seeing opportunities in the crisis, people from the Northern State brought bananas to sell on the island and accepted the island's currency. The price was 1 banana for 1 stone; an interesting detail is that in the Northern State, 1 copper buys 6 bananas.

A few days earlier, people from the Island State had decided to visit the Northern State. When they got there they were interested in some products, but they didn't have copper. So they went to the merchants who had received stones from selling bananas and exchanged stones for copper. The exchange rate was 6 stones for 1 copper, because 6 stones had the same purchasing power as 1 copper.

**Concept:** The exchange rate is a relationship between the currencies of two countries that results in the price of one measured in terms of the other.

- - -

### 4. Most of the money in circulation doesn't exist

The people of the Island State noticed that the Northern State wasn't in crisis. Knowing how inflation works - more currency in circulation than production - they decided to adopt a rule from the Northern State in their own state too.

They realized that banks create money that doesn't exist. For example, José deposits 100 stones. Maria asks for an 80-stone loan, so the bank lends her 80 stones that belong to José. In other words, 180 stones were injected into the economy, but only 100 actually exist. To avoid endless money creation, the bank must be prevented from using all of the savings, because it puts money that doesn't exist into circulation.

So the Northern State created a rule for banks: they can't lend all the money and must keep 50% of it in an account controlled by the government - that money can't be used. That way the government knows that for every 100 coins it creates, 200 are injected into the economy.
