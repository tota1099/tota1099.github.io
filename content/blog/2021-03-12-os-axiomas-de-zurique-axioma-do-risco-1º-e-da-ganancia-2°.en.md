#### THE 1st MAJOR AXIOM: ON RISK

"Worry is not a sickness but a sign of health. If you are not worried, you are not risking enough."

The book tells the story of two young women - Mary and Silvia - who had two different views. Silvia looked for a safe investment with some return, while Mary accepted taking risks to try to grow her capital significantly.

Silvia invested in a savings account and Mary in stocks. In the first years, Mary lost some money and Silvia kept her capital. But in the end, Silvia succeeded with her investments and ended up getting rich. Mary, on the other hand, not only didn't get rich, but risks not having enough money for a decent retirement.

##### THE 1st MINOR AXIOM

Always play for meaningful stakes. Betting small amounts won't make you rich. Of course you shouldn't bet everything to the point of going broke, but you should bet at levels that are worth it.

##### THE 2nd MINOR AXIOM

Resist the allure of diversification. Diversifying your investments means reducing risk, and with it the chance of getting rich. "Put your eggs in one basket, and watch that basket."

***

#### THE 2nd MAJOR AXIOM: ON GREED

"Always take your profit too soon."

Don't rush! Just because a stock's price doubled in 1 year doesn't mean it will double again! Set exit prices and, once you reach them, get out! It's better to leave early with a gain than too late and lose money.

##### THE 3rd MINOR AXIOM

Decide in advance what gain you want from a venture, and when you get it, get out! For example, I bought XPTO stock at 10.00 and set an exit price of 15.00 (a 50% gain). Once it reaches that price, I'll close my position (sell the shares) regardless of anything else. Remember that the stock price went up, but NOTHING guarantees it will keep going up, or even that it won't drop to 5.00, for example.
