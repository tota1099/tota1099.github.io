#### THE 3rd MAJOR AXIOM: ON HOPE

"When the ship starts to sink, don't pray! Jump."

It's very important to be able to accept when you've made a mistake. If you made an investment and realized it was a big mistake, having already lost 10% of it, don't wait for better times! If everything indicates the ship is going to sink, accept your 10% loss, because the longer you take to get out, the bigger your loss becomes. Knowing how to lose is one of the virtues of a good speculator.

##### THE 4th MINOR AXIOM

Accept small losses cheerfully as a fact of life. Get out and move on to the next one!

- - -

#### THE 4th MAJOR AXIOM: ON FORECASTS

"Human behavior is unpredictable! Distrust anyone who claims to know the future."

Don't pay attention to forecasts about the future, because **NOBODY** can predict the future. If they could, the famous "prophets" would already be rich just from winning the lottery.
