P/E - Price-to-Earnings

The Price/Earnings ratio works as an indicator of the optimism or pessimism investors have in the market, and it also helps identify financial opportunities.

How to interpret it?

The P/E shows how much the market is betting on the company's future. A high P/E means the market believes the company has a good future. A low P/E indicates little confidence in the company's future, but that can turn into a good opportunity.

Formula: P/E = Share Price / Earnings per Share

P/B - Price-to-Book

The Price-to-Book ratio shows whether a share price is relatively cheap or not compared to its book value.

How to interpret it?

If the P/B is below 1, it means the market is paying less for the company's shares than the company is actually worth. This may indicate a good opportunity.

Formula: P/B = Market Price / Book Value

P/B of CIA TRANSMISSÃO ENERGIA ELÉTRICA PAULISTA (TRPL4) (based on values from 05/11/2021)

Book Value = 13,307,781,000 / 658,883,304 = ~20.19

P/B = 25.3 / 20.19 = 1.25
