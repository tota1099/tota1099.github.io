### 1. Why inflation hurts the poorer classes the most

To start the discussion, we need to consider a few points about the Island State's current situation: the state's structure was a considerable part of the island's economy. The government runs a deficit, meaning it spends more than it collects, and so it has to issue more debt securities and grow its debt. When it needs to pay off its debts, the government usually prints more money.

The state doesn't produce wealth; it takes it from those who actually produce - workers and entrepreneurs - so it isn't careful with money, since it doesn't have to work to get it.

The state should cut advisors and employees, but in practice it never does, because it holds the power.

But after all, what does this have to do with inflation hurting the poor the most?

Imagine a worker earns 100 stones in salary at the end of the month and their monthly expenses are 100 stones. Now imagine the state collects 10 thousand stones in taxes per month and spends 12 thousand. It then decides to print 2 thousand more stones to cover its debt, making prices rise in the same proportion (20% inflation).

The worker will now spend 120 stones if they keep the same lifestyle. They'll have to increase their income or cut their expenses.

So who ended up with those 2 thousand newly issued stones? The state! It used them to pay its expenses.

The ones protected from inflation were the savers, because they received the interest paid on government bonds.

### 2. Capital formation and increased productivity

Recalling some concepts: if the island has only 100 bananas and 200 stones, the equilibrium price of a banana will be 2 stones.

If the state decides to print 200 more stones, so there are now 400 stones and 100 bananas, the equilibrium price goes to 4 stones.

For prices to go down, you shouldn't just take money out of circulation, but also focus on increasing the island's production.

The island's production currently comes from only 1 factor of production: labor. To produce more, we can create tools that increase production with the same workforce.

For example, the coconut pickers built a tool, a ladder, which made their work easier. Before, each worker picked 50 coconuts a day; with the ladder they're picking 200.

However, building the ladder would take the worker 3 days, and there was a risk it wouldn't make the work any easier. That's what we call risk. The higher the risk, the higher the expected return. He could have built the ladder and it might have changed nothing, but he chose to take the risk.

> **Concept: Saving**
>
> Giving up present consumption in order to consume more and better in the future.

Another way to take this risk is for the coconut picker to save part of his income until he reaches a point where spending 3 days without producing isn't a problem. This is where the 2nd factor of production comes in: capital, in this case the ladder.

Saving allows the creation of Capital to expand Production.

It's not consumption that generates Economic Growth, it's production. If we've produced 50 bananas, consumption is limited to that production.

### 3. Trade is good for everyone

The Northern State has much more developed trade than the Island State.

A mistake many people make is believing in self-sufficiency, that is, that it's good not to depend on anyone. Let's think about it with an example:

There's 1 rancher selling meat and 1 farmer selling potatoes. Both want to eat both foods.

**Scenario 1 - The farmer is better at growing potatoes, the rancher at raising cattle**

Both the Farmer and the Rancher produce potatoes and meat. The difference is that the farmer's land is better, and the Rancher is better at raising cattle.\
So it's more expensive for the farmer to raise cattle than for the rancher, and more expensive for the rancher to grow potatoes than for the farmer. In this scenario it clearly makes more sense for the rancher to raise cattle and the farmer to grow potatoes.

**Scenario 2 - The rancher is better at both raising cattle and growing potatoes**

Both work 8 hours a day and can choose to raise cattle or grow potatoes.
