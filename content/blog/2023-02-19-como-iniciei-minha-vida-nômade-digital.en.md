I've been working as a software developer since 2014, and one of the biggest perks of this profession is being able to work from anywhere in the world! Combining my professional situation with my love of traveling, the digital nomad life always appealed to me!

Then the end of 2022 arrived, along with that cliché moment of setting goals for the next year. My girlfriend and I decided on our goal: live as digital nomads for 1 year!

## January (Curitiba, PR 🇧🇷)

Our first destination was Curitiba, for a few reasons: we're from Florianópolis, Santa Catarina, and to start we wanted somewhere nice but not too far away. We knew the first trip would be full of lessons; we'd bring more clothes than we should, miss our family, among other things.

In Curitiba, we stayed from January 7, 2023 to January 28 (21 days), and visited every place we were interested in. On top of that, we were able to calmly get a feel for how the city works, walk around with no agenda and do things that aren't typical for tourists.
