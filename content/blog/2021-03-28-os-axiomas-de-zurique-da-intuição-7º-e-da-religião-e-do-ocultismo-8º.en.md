#### THE 7th MAJOR AXIOM: ON INTUITION

"A hunch can be trusted only if it can be explained."

We often have intuitions about the right thing to do. We shouldn't ignore them, because our subconscious may have discovered something we don't know yet. However, we need to be careful: intuition can be wrong.

When an intuition comes to you, be careful! A wrong intuition can be very dangerous, so it's important to test it: do you have knowledge about the subject, do you know enough about it, etc.

##### THE 11th MINOR AXIOM

Never confuse a hunch with a hope.

When we want something very much, our mind starts to believe it will happen. That's why it's common for unfounded hunches to turn into unfounded hopes.

Example: buying a painting by an unknown artist. You have a hunch that someday they'll become famous. Over time that doesn't happen, but you keep feeding your hopes.

----

#### THE 8th MAJOR AXIOM: ON RELIGION AND THE OCCULT

"It is unlikely that God's plan for the universe includes making you rich."

Relying on the supernatural is like relying on illusions of order. If you depend on God, or on any force or entity, to make your fortune, there's a good chance that one day you'll lower your guard and lose everything.

##### THE 12th MINOR AXIOM

If astrology worked, all astrologers would be rich.

This axiom is self-explanatory. Have you ever seen one of these gurus who is rich?

##### THE 13th MINOR AXIOM

A superstition need not be exorcised. It can be enjoyed, provided it is kept in its place.

You don't need to exorcise your superstition, but you can use it for other purposes, like games where only luck counts - the book's example is the "jogo do bicho", an illegal Brazilian lottery - but don't invest money based on superstitions.
